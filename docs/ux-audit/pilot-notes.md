# Pilot usability test (UX-58)

**Status: not run yet.** This needs three real salons. Each salon provides one owner and one technician, and the test runs in their language. The script below comes from §8 of `docs/UX-REVAMP-PLAN.md`. Copy the "Session" block once per participant.

## Before the visit

- Seed nothing. Use the salon's own data after one week of real use, or the demo salon if they have not started.
- Bring a phone and a tablet with the app on the Home Screen. Pair the tablet with a name like "Pilot tablet".
- Print the SUS form in English and in Vietnamese.
- Ask permission before recording. Record the screen only, never faces.

## Tasks and targets

The targets come from the click budget in §7.3 of the revamp plan. Time each task with a stopwatch as well.

## Debrief (five questions)

1. What was confusing?
2. Which word was wrong or strange? Write down the word they would use.
3. What would you change first?
4. Would you use this every day? Why or why not?
5. SUS form, ten questions, in their language.
