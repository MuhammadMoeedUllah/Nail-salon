## 1. Owner voices

**The split is a cultural constant, and so is the check/cash habit.** The Vietnamese-language trade press states it plainly: "Từ nhiều năm nay thì nguyên tắc ăn chia bất di bất dịch giữa thợ nail và chủ người Việt là 6/4 và tiền tip 100% là của người thợ" (for years the unshakeable rule between Vietnamese techs and owners has been 6/4, with 100% of tips going to the tech) and, in the same breath, "số tiền trả lương cho thợ thì bắt buộc phải là 50% tiền mặt và 50% tiền check" (pay must be 50% cash, 50% check). Source: [viethome.co.uk, "Chủ tiệm nail lời được bao nhiêu từ mỗi người thợ?"](https://viethome.co.uk/nails/nghe-nails/63699-chu-tiem-nail-duoc-loi-bao-nhieu-tu-tho). A second source repeats the norm and adds the unspoken reason: "Thường thợ Nails được trả 50% tiền check và 50% tiền mặt và số tiền mặt này có thể không khai thuế" (the cash half may go unreported). Source: [snschairs.com, "Thu nhập nghề Nail ở Mỹ"](https://snschairs.com/blogs/news/thu-nhap-nghe-nail-o-my).

**"Bao lương" is a retention tool, not just a pay basis.** One Vietnamese business blog calls it a syndrome: "Hội chứng bao lương hay còn gọi là 'hội chứng bảo an' hay 'hội chứng giữ thợ' nghĩa là người chủ Nail sẽ đưa ra một mức thu nhập cố định... nhằm mục đích giữ người thợ đó lại cho tiệm của mình" (the owner guarantees a fixed weekly income to keep the tech). It then describes the hybrid most owners actually run: "việc bao lương cũng được chủ tiệm áp dụng chung với phương thức ăn chia, nghĩa là người thợ sẽ lãnh hoa hồng nếu làm nhiều hơn số lương được bao" (guarantee, but the tech gets commission if they out-earn the guarantee). Source: [wocprint.com, "Quan hệ giữa chủ và thợ Nail"](https://wocprint.com/blog/quan-giua-chu-va-tho-nail-lam-cach-nao-de-ca-hai-cung-co-loi/). A whole article is titled around the owner's dilemma: ["Chủ Bao Lương Cho Thợ và Giải Pháp Cho Luật Thuế và Lao Động trong Nghề Nails"](https://raovatnailsalon.com/chu%CC%89-bao-luong-cho-tho%CC%A3-va-gia%CC%89i-phap-cho-luat-thue-va-lao-dong-trong-nghe-nails/) (raovatnailsalon.com).

**Owners know the audit risk and frame it as "ăn chia + 1099 = phạt".** A widely syndicated Vietnamese explainer says: "Trong mấy năm gần đây, do các đợt thanh tra của cơ quan lao động và kiểm toán của sở thuế IRS nhắm vào các tiệm nail của người Việt tại hầu hết các tiểu bang, rất nhiều chủ tiệm đã bị phạt nặng vì áp dụng lối ăn chia và cấp mẫu 1099-MISC cho thợ tự đóng thuế" (many owners were fined heavily for running revenue splits and issuing 1099-MISC). Sources: [baonail.com, "Nghề Nail với Luật Thuế Vụ và Lao Động"](https://baonail.com/news.php?tid=311); [nguoiviet.us, "Nghề Nail ở Mỹ: Luật thuế và luật lao động cần biết"](https://nguoiviet.us/nghe-nail-o-my-luat-thue-va-luat-lao-dong-can-biet/); [vietbao.com, "Hoa Kỳ: Nghề Nail và Luật Thuế Vụ Liên Bang"](https://vietbao.com/a264609/hoa-ky-nghe-nail-va-luat-thue-vu-lien-bang). The same sources state the baseline owners are told: every "employee" must get minimum wage and "lương giờ phụ trội (overtime)" in all states. Source: [dautumy.us, "Luật lao động Hoa Kỳ và vấn đề lương bổng của thợ Nail"](https://www.dautumy.us/news/579/luat-lao-dong-hoa-ky-va-van-de-luong-bong-cua-tho-nail).

**The enforcement stories owners share.** Three Rhode Island salons and their owner agreed to pay $753,500; DOL found they "did not pay proper overtime... did not maintain accurate work records, provided false information to investigators and compelled employees to sign documents that contained false information" ([US DOL, 29 May 2024](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/sol/sol20240529)). In NYC, DOL named the pattern the app must replace: workers "typically paid a flat rate - in cash - for each day they work, in an attempt to avoid both paying overtime and keeping accurate time records" ([US DOL WHD, 2016](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/whd/whd20160407-0)); Nassau/Suffolk salons paid $203K to 95 workers ([US DOL WHD, 2016](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/whd/whd20160824)); Massachusetts litigated the same issues ([Salem Nail Bar v. Fair Labor Div.](https://www.mass.gov/decision/salem-nail-bar-v-fair-labor-div-lb-22-0441-445-tentative-decision)). The Vietnamese press recently ran "CHẤN ĐỘNG CỘNG ĐỒNG NAIL MỸ: chủ hệ thống hơn 60 tiệm nail nhận tội trốn thuế, thiệt hại ít nhất 32 triệu USD", with unreported wages from 2016 to 2024 ([congdongmy.com](https://congdongmy.com/chan-dong-cong-dong-nail-my-chu-he-thong-hon-60-tiem-nail-nhan-toi-tron-thue-thiet-hai-it-nhat-32-trieu-usd/)).

**Wage bonds (New York).** Since the 6 Oct 2015 order, NY salons must post a wage bond scaled to headcount: $25,000 for 2-5 full-time-equivalent nail workers, $40,000 for 6-10, $75,000 for 11-25, $125,000 above 25; failing to carry it costs $500 then up to $2,500 plus license suspension. Sources: [SuretyBonds.com NY wage payment bond](https://www.suretybonds.com/states/new-york/wage-payment-bond); [Governor's office announcement](https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-cuomo-announces-wage-bond-requirements-nail-salon-owners-and-availability-new-nail). Owners organised against it: ["New York Nail Salon Owners Plan Lawsuit Over 'Wage Bond' Requirement"](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/asian-america/new-york-nail-salon-owners-plan-lawsuit-over-wage-bond-n426911) (NBC). Design note: the bond tiers are a headcount function, so the app's active-technician count is a number an NY owner will want to see.

**Mainstream trade press.** Nails Magazine records that 50% commission "was the standard method throughout the '80s and '90s" without leaving owners a solid bottom line ([NAILS, "The Big Payoff"](https://www.nailsmag.com/392824/the-big-payoff); [NAILS, "Is Your Pay Structure The Problem?"](https://www.nailsmag.com/390752/is-your-pay-structure-the-problem)). GlossGenius puts today's US norm at hourly or "40 to 55 percent of the service revenue they bring in - plus tips", often starting new hires hourly and moving them to 50% ([GlossGenius](https://glossgenius.com/blog/how-do-nail-salons-pay-their-employees)). Salon-sale listings show the real mix: "60/40 split... technicians keep 60%" and "approximately $300/week in base compensation in addition to their commission" ([BizQuest BW2491782](https://www.bizquest.com/business-for-sale/absentee-owned-nail-salon-80k-profit-30-years-established/BW2491782); [BizQuest BW2415702](https://www.bizquest.com/business-for-sale/140k-owner-income-25-year-boca-raton-nail-salon/BW2415702)). Owner threads exist at [SalonGeek](https://www.salongeek.com/threads/nails-salon-owners-how-do-you-pay-your-technicians.335893/) and [This Ugly Beauty Business, "The Great Commission Failure"](https://thisuglybeautybusiness.com/2014/04/the-great-commission-failure-tale-of.html) (not retrievable today).

**Turnover.** The "bao lương" articles frame guaranteed pay as "hội chứng giữ thợ" (the keep-the-tech syndrome): owners over-pay guarantees because techs walk to the salon across the street offering a better split ([wocprint.com](https://wocprint.com/blog/quan-giua-chu-va-tho-nail-lam-cach-nao-de-ca-hai-cung-co-loi/)). UCLA notes the split moved against workers after COVID, from 60% to 50% in some shops ([LAist](https://laist.com/brief/news/california-nail-salons-minimum-wage-ucla-healthy-collaborative)).

**Not retrieved (follow-up):** Reddit threads (no results for five phrasings), YouTube "chủ tiệm nail trả lương thợ" videos, Pro Nails Association posts.

## 2. Technician voices

**Who the techs are.** UCLA's national study: 85% women, 84% Asian, 81% foreign-born, and of those 82% born in Vietnam ([UCLA Labor Center](https://labor.ucla.edu/ucla-releases-first-national-study-of-labor-conditions-in-nail-salon-industry/)). In California the 2021 median hourly wage was estimated at $10.94, below the $13 small-business minimum, because techs are "paid by the manicure instead of on an hourly basis" ([UCLA press advisory](https://labor.ucla.edu/press-advisory/californias-fast-growing-nail-salon-workforce-faces-low-wages-misclassification-says-new-research-from-ucla-chnsc); [LAist](https://laist.com/brief/news/california-nail-salons-minimum-wage-ucla-healthy-collaborative); [ABC7](https://abc7.com/nail-salon-industry-ucla-labor-center-study-poor-working-conditions-low-pay/14547140/)). Workers report "tip docking" and a post-COVID slide from keeping 60% to 50%; 65% are rent-burdened and 15% receive SNAP (same sources). CalMatters covers techs caught between piece-rate custom and California's hourly rule ([CalMatters, 2025](https://calmatters.org/politics/2025/07/workers-nail-salons-labor-bill/)).

**Wage theft is the lived default in New York.** The NY Nail Salon Workers Association surveyed 98 women across the five boroughs, Long Island, Westchester, Dutchess and Putnam: "Four out of five... experienced wage theft, with an average loss of $181 per week, or $9,412 per year", and the association estimates 46% are wrongly treated as independent contractors. Sources: [Gothamist](https://gothamist.com/news/majority-nyc-nail-salon-workers-still-face-wage-theft-new-report-finds); [CBS New York](https://cbsnews.com/newyork/news/nail-salon-workers-association-wage-theft-report); [Perfect Union](https://perfectunion.us/nail-salon-workers-are-exploited-a-new-york-bill-could-change-that/). Adhikaar (Nepali-speaking workers' centre) reports groups of workers arriving to complain of unpaid wages and then being "fired without pay". Source: [Chronogram](https://www.chronogram.com/river-newsroom/nail-salon-workers-are-transforming-their-industry-after-the-pandemic-18194723/); organiser interviews at [New Labor Forum](https://newlaborforum.cuny.edu/2018/01/17/justice-on-the-job-for-nail-salon-workers/). Workers describe "low wages, non-existent benefits, erratic schedules" ([The Chief](https://thechiefleader.com/stories/nail-salon-workers-seek-hands-on-approach-for-better-pay,44828)).

**Thợ bao lương vs thợ ăn chia, in the tech's own frame.** The Vietnamese sources above define the two tech types: the "thợ bao lương" is guaranteed a weekly figure (e.g. "bao $900/tuần"), and the "thợ ăn chia" lives on the 6/4 split plus 100% of tips ([viethome.co.uk](https://viethome.co.uk/nails/nghe-nails/63699-chu-tiem-nail-duoc-loi-bao-nhieu-tu-tho); [wocprint.com](https://wocprint.com/blog/quan-giua-chu-va-tho-nail-lam-cach-nao-de-ca-hai-cung-co-loi/)). The tip rule "tiền tip 100% là của người thợ" is the single line techs repeat most, which is why the UCLA finding of tip docking lands as betrayal rather than accounting.

**The tech's notebook vs the owner's count. [background, verify]** Techs on a split keep their own tally (a paper stub per client, or a phone note "date - service - price - tip") because the split is computed from a ticket list they never see totalled. Vendors sell against exactly this friction: "eliminating disputes" ([Tilavon](https://tilavon.com/guides/manage-salon-employees)); the Vietnamese-market app "Tính Lương Thợ Nail" promises to "tự động tính toán, lưu trữ hồ sơ và in phiếu check-in để tiện theo dõi" (auto-calculate, keep records, print a check-in slip) ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/t%C3%ADnh-l%C6%B0%C6%A1ng-th%E1%BB%A3-nail/id6741119182?l=vi)). DOL's finding that owners "compelled employees to sign documents that contained false information" ([DOL 2024](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/sol/sol20240529)) is why the tech's statement must come from the same records the owner keeps.

**What a tech would want on a stub (synthesised from the above):** the list of their own tickets by day; card tips and cash tips on separate lines, both at 100%; the guarantee or split rule written out ("bao $900" or "6/4"); hours in and out; and a Vietnamese version they can read without asking the owner. Direct tech quotes from Reddit and forums are a follow-up item (not retrieved today).

## 3. Bookkeeper / CPA voices

**What the CPA asks for.** Salon-specialist firms list the inputs: "employee information (names, addresses, Social Security numbers), hours worked, pay rates, tips received, commissions earned, and payroll taxes withheld" ([XOA TAX](https://www.xoatax.com/nail-salon-payroll-guide/)); "a weekly routine of documenting all income and expenses on a specific day" ([CSI Accounting](https://blog.csiaccounting.com/5-bookkeeping-tips-for-your-salon)); and reconciling "card, cash, Zelle, and app deposits... every month with real numbers, not guesses" ([Atif CPA](https://atifcpa.com/blog/nail-salon-business-guide-taxes-finance-growth-tips/)). Other salon-niche CPAs: [Meru](https://www.meruaccounting.com/our-clients/bookkeeping-for-nail-salons/), [man.cpa](https://man.cpa/nail), [Boyum](https://myboyum.com/industries-we-serve/salons/), and in Vietnamese [Tax Viet, "Thuế Tiệm Nail"](https://www.taxviet.co/blog/tiem-nail-va-thue). The field format is a weekly Excel sheet; Vietnamese software ads target owners who want to "bỏ hẳn bảng tính Excel" ([hoinailviet.com](https://hoinailviet.com/kien-thuc-nganh-nail/phan-mem-quan-ly-tiem-nail/)).

**Cash tips and the IRS, verified scope.** Employees must report cash tips of $20 or more in a month to the employer, and tips (cash and card) must be included on W-2s ([XOA TAX](https://www.xoatax.com/nail-salon-payroll-guide/); [IRS Publication 531](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p531)). Form 4070 ("Employee's Report of Tips to Employer") is the generic monthly report any tipped employee may use; **Form 8027 and allocated tips apply only to "large food or beverage establishments"**, so a nail salon does not file 8027 and the 8%-of-receipts allocation rule does not apply ([Patriot Software](https://www.patriotsoftware.com/blog/payroll/what-is-form-8027/); [Gusto](https://gusto.com/resources/articles/taxes/form-8027); [1099fire](https://www.1099fire.com/blog/irs-form-8027-vs-form-4070-what-restaurant-employees-and-owners-need-to-know/)). Implication: the app should produce a monthly per-tech tip total that can stand in for the 4070 report, and should not mention 8027.

**Payroll providers.** Salons that run formal payroll use Gusto (often through Vagaro's integration), ADP RUN, Paychex, or GlossGenius Payroll; vendors sell "payroll-ready CSV exports for QuickBooks, Gusto, and ADP" with tips "separated by cash and card", and warn that "mixing up the classification [W-2 vs 1099] carries real IRS risk". Sources: [Homebase, "Best Salon Software with Payroll"](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/salon-software-with-payroll); [Zenoti commission/payroll guide](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/best-salon-commission-payroll-software); [Rich Payment Solutions](https://richpaymentsolutions.com/point-of-sale-system-for-nail-salon-tip-payroll/); [GlossGenius Payroll](https://glossgenius.com/payroll).

## 4. Daily rhythm of a salon

- **Hours and peaks. [background, verify]** Strip-mall salons typically open 9:30-10:00 and close 19:00-19:30 Monday-Saturday, with shorter Sunday hours; Friday, Saturday and the days before holidays are peak. Vietnamese owner guides describe the daily cash-up as listing "tiền mặt trong két thu ngân, tiền khách trả bằng thẻ, tiền khách tip" (cash in the drawer, card takings, tips) in a ledger each night ([IDTEK](https://idtek.ca/tin-tuc/quan-ly-hieu-qua/4-tip-quan-ly-doanh-thu-danh-cho-cac-chu-tiem-nail)).
- **When pay is computed.** DOL's description of the cash day-rate model ([DOL 2016](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/whd/whd20160407-0)) and the "bao lương mỗi tuần" language ([wocprint.com](https://wocprint.com/blog/quan-giua-chu-va-tho-nail-lam-cach-nao-de-ca-hai-cung-co-loi/)) both point to a weekly cycle; the owner sits down after Sunday close or Monday morning with the week's tickets. Formal-payroll salons run biweekly through Gusto/ADP ([Homebase](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/salon-software-with-payroll)). The app should default to a weekly workweek that ends Sunday, with biweekly as an option.
- **How a service is recorded.** Three coexisting systems: paper ticket slips, a "turn" board, and the POS. Turn systems rotate walk-ins fairly; vendors describe "half-turn and full-turn rotation" ([Supaday](https://supaday.app/nail-salon-software)) and a board shown "on a TV in the breakroom so everyone knows who's next" ([ABTurns](https://www.abturns.com/)). The Vietnamese payroll app prints a "phiếu check-in" per ticket ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/t%C3%ADnh-l%C6%B0%C6%A1ng-th%E1%BB%A3-nail/id6741119182?l=vi)).
- **Who holds the tablet.** The owner or a front-desk person; "the most common setup is an iPad or Android tablet at the front desk, paired with a card reader" ([Zenoti](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/best-nail-salon-pos-systems)). Kiosk vendors assume "any iPad or Android tablet you already have" with check-in under 30 seconds ([Certxa](https://certxa.com/checkin-kiosk); [DaySmart KIOSK](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/-/id1482427587)). Expect older iPads and sub-$150 Android tablets; plan for both.
- **Wi-Fi and lighting. [background, verify]** Shop Wi-Fi is consumer-grade and shared with clients, so punches must queue offline. Front desks sit under white LED panels or beside glass storefronts, so clock-in photos face backlight; capture a small frame and show a preview.

## 5. UX implications (design rules, each tied to a source)

1. **Show cash tips and card tips on separate lines, both at 100% to the tech.** "Tiền tip 100% là của người thợ" ([viethome.co.uk](https://viethome.co.uk/nails/nghe-nails/63699-chu-tiem-nail-duoc-loi-bao-nhieu-tu-tho)); vendors already separate them for payroll ([Rich Payment Solutions](https://richpaymentsolutions.com/point-of-sale-system-for-nail-salon-tip-payroll/)).
2. **Use the owner's words: "bao lương", "ăn chia", "6/4", "turn", "check/cash".** They are the industry's actual vocabulary ([snschairs.com](https://snschairs.com/blogs/news/thu-nhap-nghe-nail-o-my); [wocprint.com](https://wocprint.com/blog/quan-giua-chu-va-tho-nail-lam-cach-nao-de-ca-hai-cung-co-loi/)).
3. **Model "guarantee vs commission, whichever is higher" as a first-class pay basis**, because that is how bao lương is actually run ([wocprint.com](https://wocprint.com/blog/quan-giua-chu-va-tho-nail-lam-cach-nao-de-ca-hai-cung-co-loi/)).
4. **The statement must list the tech's own tickets by day so they can reconcile against their notebook.** Wage theft averages $181/week and is discovered by comparison ([Gothamist](https://gothamist.com/news/majority-nyc-nail-salon-workers-still-face-wage-theft-new-report-finds)).
5. **Generate the tech statement and the owner's binder from the same records; never let either be typed separately.** DOL cited owners who had workers sign false documents ([DOL 2024](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/sol/sol20240529)).
6. **Hours in/out must exist even for day-rate and commission techs.** The core violation is a flat cash day rate with no time records ([DOL 2016](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/whd/whd20160407-0)).
7. **Show the minimum-wage top-up as its own line, computed from hours, not hidden in the split.** Median $10.94/hour against a $13 floor is the problem to make visible ([LAist](https://laist.com/brief/news/california-nail-salons-minimum-wage-ucla-healthy-collaborative)).
8. **PIN pad: four large digits, no keyboard, under three seconds, usable with damp hands between clients.** Front-desk kiosks are benchmarked at under 30 seconds for a full client check-in ([Certxa](https://certxa.com/checkin-kiosk)); a staff punch must be far faster.
9. **Default workweek: weekly, ending Sunday; biweekly optional.** Weekly "bao lương mỗi tuần" custom vs biweekly payroll providers ([wocprint.com](https://wocprint.com/blog/quan-giua-chu-va-tho-nail-lam-cach-nao-de-ca-hai-cung-co-loi/); [Homebase](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/salon-software-with-payroll)).
10. **Record the payment method per pay run (check, cash, payroll) without judgement, and keep the total in one place.** The 50/50 check-cash habit is universal ([viethome.co.uk](https://viethome.co.uk/nails/nghe-nails/63699-chu-tiem-nail-duoc-loi-bao-nhieu-tu-tho)); the record is what protects the owner if the IRS asks ([congdongmy.com](https://congdongmy.com/chan-dong-cong-dong-nail-my-chu-he-thong-hon-60-tiem-nail-nhan-toi-tron-thue-thiet-hai-it-nhat-32-trieu-usd/)).
11. **Monthly per-tech tip total, labelled as the employee tip report; never mention Form 8027.** 8027 is for large food/beverage establishments only ([Patriot Software](https://www.patriotsoftware.com/blog/payroll/what-is-form-8027/)); the $20/month rule is what applies ([XOA TAX](https://www.xoatax.com/nail-salon-payroll-guide/)).
12. **Bookkeeper export = one CSV with the fields CPAs list** (name, hours, rate, commission, tips, deductions), in Gusto/QuickBooks/ADP column order ([XOA TAX](https://www.xoatax.com/nail-salon-payroll-guide/); [Zenoti](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/best-salon-commission-payroll-software)).
13. **Show the W-2/1099 status of every technician on the settings screen with a plain warning**, because "ăn chia + 1099" is the named failure mode ([baonail.com](https://baonail.com/news.php?tid=311)) and the classification mix-up "carries real IRS risk" ([Homebase](https://www.joinhomebase.com/blog/salon-software-with-payroll)).
14. **Works on an old iPad and a cheap Android tablet, in a browser, offline-tolerant.** Front desks run either ([Zenoti](https://www.zenoti.com/thecheckin/best-nail-salon-pos-systems); [Certxa](https://certxa.com/checkin-kiosk)).
15. **Ticket entry in two taps: tech, then service; price pre-filled, tips typed last.** Salons already run turn boards and slips at this speed ([Supaday](https://supaday.app/nail-salon-software); [ABTurns](https://www.abturns.com/)).
16. **Every edit keeps who/when/why and the before-value, and the tech's statement footnotes voided or edited tickets.** This is the inverse of "provided false information to investigators" ([DOL 2024](https://www.dol.gov/newsroom/releases/sol/sol20240529)).
17. **Show active-technician headcount on the NY owner's dashboard**, since wage-bond tiers break at 5, 10 and 25 ([SuretyBonds.com](https://www.suretybonds.com/states/new-york/wage-payment-bond)).
18. **Vietnamese first on the statement for the tech, English first for the owner and CPA**, selectable per person. 64% of Vietnamese immigrants speak English less than very well ([MPI](https://www.migrationpolicy.org/article/language-diversity-and-english-proficiency-united-states)).
19. **Never silently apply the 7(i) retail commission exemption or any "the split covers overtime" shortcut.** Commission "regardless of the number of hours they worked" is exactly what courts have scrutinised ([NBC, "Quietly Suffering"](https://www.nbcnews.com/news/amp/ncna704116)).

## 6. Accessibility and language

- **English proficiency.** About 64% of Vietnamese immigrants aged 5+ spoke English less than "very well" in 2022, versus 46% of all immigrants ([Migration Policy Institute](https://www.migrationpolicy.org/article/language-diversity-and-english-proficiency-united-states)); 57% of people who speak Vietnamese at home were LEP in 2019 ACS data ([Census ACS-50](https://www.census.gov/content/dam/Census/library/publications/2022/acs/acs-50.pdf)); 40.8% of Vietnamese New Yorkers were LEP vs 22.4% citywide ([Asian American Federation](https://www.aafederation.org/research/vietnamese-in-nyc-a-profile/)). Owners are usually more fluent than techs but often prefer Vietnamese for money matters; the CPA is usually English-first or bilingual. Design for a bilingual screen, not a language toggle buried in settings.
- **Fonts and diacritics.** Vietnamese stacks tone marks over vowels with existing diacritics (ấ, ở, ữ); poorly chosen fonts drop or collide marks, and buttons truncate badly ([Seatongue localisation guide](https://seatongue.com/resources/language-center/vietnamese-localization-guide/)). Use a stack with verified Vietnamese coverage (Noto Sans, Segoe UI, Roboto, Inter) and test on cheap Android tablets; see [vietnamesetypography.com, Type Recommendations](https://vietnamesetypography.com/type-recommendations/) and [Wikipedia, Vietnamese language and computers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vietnamese_language_and_computers). Leave 20-30% more vertical room in buttons for stacked marks, and never set Vietnamese in all-caps with tight leading.
- **Numbers, dates, currency. [background, verify]** US nail-industry Vietnamese keeps US formats even in Vietnamese UI: `$1,234.56` with a period decimal (not the Vietnam-standard `1.234,56 đ`), dates as `10/07/2026` (MM/DD) or spelled "7 tháng 10", 12-hour clock with am/pm, Fahrenheit, miles. Do not localise the number format to vi-VN; use `en-US` number formatting with Vietnamese labels.
- **Weekdays and weeks. [background, verify]** Weekdays are ordinal: Thứ Hai (Mon) through Thứ Bảy (Sat), Chủ Nhật (Sun); abbreviations T2-T7 and CN are universally understood. "Tuần này" (this week), "tuần trước" (last week), "cuối tuần" (weekend). Salon people say "tuần làm việc" for workweek but will read "workweek" too. Months are numbered: "tháng 10" or "T10". In Vietnamese UI the US week still starts Sunday on calendars, but salon pay weeks usually run Monday-Sunday; make the start day a setting and print it on every statement.
- **Reading level.** Short noun phrases on buttons ("Vào ca", "Ra ca", "Thêm phiếu"); many techs read English only at sight-word level, so an icon plus a Vietnamese word beats an English sentence.
